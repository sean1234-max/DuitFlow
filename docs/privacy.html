DuitFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

What DuitFlow does

DuitFlow helps you record and understand your personal finances: transactions, budgets, savings goals and monthly estimates. DuitFlow does not connect to your bank and never asks for your bank login.

Information we handle

  • Records you enter: transaction amounts, types, categories, dates and notes; budgets; savings goals; repeating transactions; custom category names.
  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address and a password, which is stored by our authentication provider in hashed form.
  • Bank messages you paste: when you use "Fill from bank SMS", the text is read on your phone to fill in the form. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Questions to DuitFlow AI (only if you use it): your question, recent conversation, and a summary of your records.

Where your information is stored

  • Without an account: your records stay on your phone only.
  • With an account: your records are also stored with Supabase, our cloud database provider, so you can back them up and use more than one phone. Access is restricted so that each account can only read its own records.

DuitFlow AI

When you ask DuitFlow AI a question, your question and a summary of your records (monthly totals, budgets, goals and recent transactions) are sent to Anthropic, our AI provider, to generate the answer. AI answers can be wrong and are not financial advice.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't show ads or share your records with advertisers.
  • We don't read SMS messages or notifications on your phone; you choose what to paste.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Export transactions (CSV).
  • Sign out: removes your records from that phone; they stay in your account.
  • Delete account: Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all records stored in the cloud.
